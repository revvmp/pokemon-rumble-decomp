## F-341-1: Boundary and complete CHBMParam

Its first eleven functions are the preceding errmsg.cpp tail: the initializer at 0x801E20E0 registers the destructor at 0x801E1CE0 for its global object. The next function, 0x801E2110, returns zero and is stored into HBMDataInfo::sound_callback by 0x801E24C0. The following accessors feed the same Home Button resource loader.
