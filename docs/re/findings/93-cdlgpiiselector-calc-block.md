# CDlgPiiSelector update and draw block (#93)

Target `wpse-v1`, immutable `orig/wpse-v1/main.dol`, analysed in the linked `build/wpse-v1/main.elf`, which verifies byte-identical. Issue #93 lists 27 functions in `.text` `0x80093540`–`0x80093F64`. All of them belong to `game/CDlgPiiSelector.cpp`, whose split, object line and first function belong to #91 ([F-91-1](91-cdlgpiiselector.md)). The file is compiled without inlining ([F-92-1](92-cdlgpiiselector-construction.md#f-92-1-the-file-is-compiled-without-inlining)).

## F-93-2: reconstruction of eight functions

`src/game/CDlgPiiSelector.cpp` now declares `CControllerList`, `MultiPlayerData` up to the player count, and `CalcEntrance` and `AssignControllers` as inline members of `CDlgPiiSelector`, with `include/game/CPlayer.h` and `include/game/ControllerTable.h` for the two outside functions. `config/wpse-v1/symbols.txt` gives the eight functions and the two outside functions the mangled names the source produces.

These members are not emitted yet. Everything in the file is in the anonymous namespace, and its only callers are `Calc` and `ShowMultiPlayerEntrance`, so the compiler drops them while those virtuals are undefined, and the object, the objdiff report (31/530 at 100%) and the linked DOL are unchanged. To check them, a scratch build added a call to `CalcEntrance` from the factory. All eight functions then compiled to the original instructions at `0x80093A10`, `0x80093A70`, `0x80093B20`, `0x80093B40`, `0x80093C50`, `0x80093CD0`, `0x80093DA0` and `0x800936E0`, apart from relocation words. They were also emitted in the original depth-first order: `CalcEntrance`, `operator->`, `AssignControllers`, the constructor, `Collect`, `AddType`, `Add`, `Get`. The scratch call is not committed. Two shape details matter. The constructor must clear the count before a four-pass loop over the pointers. `Collect` must store each pad pointer before testing it, and clear the slot again when the pad is not connected.

## F-93-3: what blocks the rest of the block

The remaining 19 functions are emitted only from `Draw`, `CalcView`, `CalcMtx` and `Calc`, and each of those needs types that other issues own:

- `CUIManager` and the other UI members of `CDataPerPlayer` (#327 and #328). `CDataPerPlayer::Draw`, `CalcView`, `CalcMtx` and `Calc` call virtuals through them, and the members before them need `CPiiListFilterSort` (#173) and the `CUIPiiListItem` vector ([F-93-4](#f-93-4-calcview-calcmtx-and-their-helpers)).
- Resolved by F-93-4: `CLayout`, held by value at `MultiPlayerData` + 0x88, now has a header, and `IDlgMsgWin` has a minimal one. `IGameMap` (#116 and the `CGameMap.cpp` blocks) through slot 29, and `INoticeDisplay` for `Draw`. The binding scan below names only some of the 25 `IGameMap` slots from 5 to 29.
- `0x80093F80`, `0x80093F70` and `0x80094240` are in #94, and `0x8009BD80` (`reset`) is in a later block.

A scan of every caller of the map getter `0x800AA2B0` names some `IGameMap` slots. It reads the `lwz r12, X(r12)` that follows the getter before `r3` is reloaded. Slot 7 is called by `EnableCalcWorld`, `IsCalcWorldEnabled` and the effect finalizers, and `CGameMap` returns its `BWorld` (member 0x40). Slot 9 is called by `ShowMiniMap`, slot 10 by the selector's entrance bindings ([F-91-2](91-cdlgpiiselector.md#f-91-2-factory-and-session-interface)), slot 15 by the notice-display bindings, slot 16 by the money and life display bindings, slot 17 by `ShowBRDisplay` and `HideBRDisplay`, slot 19 by the layout bindings (`NewLayout`, `method_layout*`), and slots 36 to 40 by the timer bindings. `Pause` and `Resume` call slot 29 with `true` and `false`, and `IsPausing` calls slot 30, both through a second getter `0x800AA2F0`. Slots 5, 6, 8, 11 to 14, 18 and 20 to 28 have no binding evidence yet. No `IGameMap` header is added here.

## F-93-4: CalcView, CalcMtx and their helpers

`CDlgPiiSelector::CalcView` (`0x80093700`) and `CalcMtx` (`0x800937E0`) now compile to the original instructions, with the four helpers they emit: the boolean conversions of `shared_ptr<CDataPerPlayer>` (`0x80093640`) and `shared_ptr<MultiPlayerData>` (`0x800936C0`), and `operator->` of `shared_ptr<CDataPerPlayer>` (`0x800936B0`) and `shared_ptr<IDlgMsgWin>` (`0x800936F0`). With `shared_ptr<MultiPlayerData>::operator->` (`0x800936E0`), which they also call, seven #93 functions are at 100% in objdiff, and the unit reports 40/530. The full build verifies.

Both functions forward their slot to each set `CDataPerPlayer`, then, while `MultiPlayerData` is set, to the dialog at 0x170. Neither calls the `CLayout` at 0x88, which only `Draw` and `Calc` reach. `MultiPlayerData` now declares `CLayout mLayout` at 0x88 and `shared_ptr<IDlgMsgWin> mMsgWin` at 0x170. The compiled offset 0x170 confirms that the `CLayout` header is 0xE8 bytes, as [F-141](141-cmoneylifedisplay-cmsgballoon.md) measured. `include/game/IDlgMsgWin.h` declares the interface with only its inline destructor (`0x80084EE0`, slot 0 of the vtable `0x8039DC90`). That vtable repeats the `IDlg` slots and adds 17 pure slots; none of them is called here, so none is declared. The class belongs to `CDlgMsgWin.cpp` (#73), whose owner can add them.

The vtable decides what is emitted. CodeWarrior emits a class's vtable with its first virtual that is neither inline nor pure. In the original every `CDlgPiiSelector` virtual is defined inline, so the vtable comes with the constructor, and `Draw`, `CalcView`, `CalcMtx` and `Calc` follow in reverse declaration order, as at `0x80093550` to `0x800938C0`. While `Calc` was only declared, the vtable stayed an undefined reference, and nothing it lists was compiled. The source now declares the undefined virtuals `inline`, to be defined later in the file. That makes the vtable local, so `CalcView`, `CalcMtx` and the implicit destructor are emitted, and `Calc`, `Draw` and the selector slots 7 to 13 remain undefined references until their blocks define them. The destructor (`0x8009E060`) and the `~shared_ptr<MultiPlayerData>` it calls (`0x8009BC10`, referenced nowhere else) are also at 100% after naming that callee.

`CDataPerPlayer::CalcView` (`0x800937A0`) and `CalcMtx` (`0x80093880`) are declared and named but not defined. Each calls its slot through the `shared_ptr<CUIManager>` at 0x84, so a definition needs the members before it. The constructor `0x800952B0` stores a container at 0x14 (constructor `0x80092480`, destructor `0x8008A450`, the `CUIPiiListItem` vector of [F-82-3](82-cdlgpiilist.md#f-82-3-implementation-object-and-remaining-dependencies)), `CPiiListFilterSort` by value at 0x20 (constructor `0x800E5D00`, destructor `0x800E5D20`), two float pairs at 0x40 and 0x48 (`0x80095480`), `bool`s at 0x50 to 0x52, three shared pointers at 0x54, 0x5C and 0x64 (destructors `0x8009BAC0` and `0x8009B970`), words at 0x6C to 0x74, and three words at 0x78 to 0x80 that all receive the `.sdata` address `r13 - 0x7554`. The destructor `0x8009AC30` also calls three member routines (`0x80099210`, `0x800992C0`, `0x80099360`) before destroying the members. `CPiiListFilterSort` is the same blocker as in F-82-3: two of its members have no evidenced type.

## F-93-5: Draw, Calc and the CDataPerPlayer members

The remaining 19 functions of #93 are reconstructed and all 27 functions of the block (`0x80093540`-`0x80093F64`) are at 100% in objdiff, with no relocation difference left. `CDlgPiiSelector::Draw` (`0x80093550`) and `Calc` (`0x800938C0`) are now defined in the class body, in the original reverse-declaration order. That emits `CalcEntrance`, `AssignControllers` and the `CControllerList` members of [F-93-2](#f-93-2-reconstruction-of-eight-functions) with no scratch call, and they match as before. `Draw` returns when the `INoticeDisplay` of the current map is shown (`GetNoticeDisplay` is `IGameMap` slot 15, whose inline default is the weak copy at `0x80093630`), then draws each set `CDataPerPlayer`, the `CLayout` and the message window. `Calc` calls `UpdateEntrance` (`0x80094240`, #94) while `MultiPlayerData` is set, runs each player's `Calc`, and when `IsFinished` (`0x80093F80`, #94) is true resets the pointer (`shared_ptr<CDataPerPlayer>::reset`, `0x8009BD80`, which calls `swap` `0x8009DBF0`), clears `mIsPausing` through `IGameMap::SetPausing(false)` (slot 29) and calls `UpdateDlgOpenState`.

`CDataPerPlayer` now has its members up to the `CFiber` at 0xD8, in the order of the constructor `0x800952B0` ([F-95-1](95-cdlgpiiselector-dataperplayer-block.md)): `IDlgPiiSelectorInternal*` at 0, the state at 4, the player index at 8, a vector of `shared_ptr<CUIPiiListItem>` at 0x14, `CPiiListFilterSort` at 0x20, `shared_ptr<CUIManager>` at 0x84, `shared_ptr<CUIPiiSpec>` at 0x9C, `shared_ptr<CUIPiiList>` at 0xA4, `shared_ptr<CUIPlayerID>` at 0xAC, the controller pointer at 0xC4, its disconnect serial at 0xC8, the `bool` at 0xCC and the `CFiber`. The virtual destructor is declared last, which puts the vtable pointer at 0x448 as in the original. The 8 bytes at 0xBC are a `VEC2` because the constructor does not initialise them. Members whose purpose is not established keep `Reserved` names and their offsets: 0xC, 0x40, 0x48, 0x50, 0x52, 0x54 to 0x80, 0x8C, 0x94, 0xB4, 0xBC and 0xD0. The `bool` at 0x10 and the `bool` at 0x51 are named after [F-96-2](96-cdlgpiiselector-block.md), whose pull request is open, and the types of 0x54 to 0x64 (`shared_ptr<BUI>`) are placeholders that only fix the 8-byte stride; no #93 function reads any of them.

`CDataPerPlayer::Calc` (`0x80093DB0`) sets the `bool` when the player's controller changed or its `GetDisconnectSerial` (`IController` slot 12) differs from the stored one, updates the `CUIPlayerID` while the flag is clear, calls `UpdateSelection` (`CUIList` slot 13) on the `CUIPiiList`, passes the selected item's `CPiiPersonalData` by value to slot 13 of the `CUIPiiSpec` with the player index, calls `Calc` on the `CUIManager` and resumes the `CFiber`. The selected-item `shared_ptr` must live in its own block: the original destroys it before the `CUIManager` call. The cast of the selected `IUIListItem` to `CUIPiiListItem` is `static_pointer_cast` (`0x8008FE10`, no runtime type check in the code), then `->` (`0x8008BA60`) and `GetPPD` (`0x8008BA50`, the `shared_ptr<CPiiPersonalData>` at +8, copied to a temporary for the by-value argument).

New headers, each limited to what the callers need: `include/game/CUIPiiListItem.h` (0x1C bytes, [F-173-3](173-cpiilistfiltersort.md)), `CUIPiiList.h` (an empty `CUIList` subclass, so slot 13 is `UpdateSelection`), `CUIPiiSpec.h` (`BUILayout` plus one new virtual, `SetPii`, which is slot 13 because `BUILayout` ends at slot 12 and the vtable `0x803AF620` overrides only inherited slots below it) and `CUIPlayerID.h` (`BUILayout` plus two words at 0x134 and 0x138, non-virtual `Update`, `0x8011F9C0`). The members of `CUIPiiSpec` (0x188 bytes) are not declared; #217 owns them. No existing header changed, so no other unit's output changed. The names `Draw`, `Calc`, `IsDisconnected`, `UpdateEntrance`, the `shared_ptr` accessors and the outside callees are in `config/wpse-v1/symbols.txt`; `UpdateEntrance` is a descriptive name for the #94 function at `0x80094240` and may be renamed by its owner.

## Method

GNU objdump 2.42 on `build/wpse-v1/main.elf` and the rebuilt object, the generated assembly under `build/wpse-v1/asm/`, Wii/1.1 with `cflags_game` through `ninja` and `tools/re/unitdiff.py`, and a Python word comparison that skips relocated and branch words (`93-cmp.py` and `93-mapslots.py` in the session scratch directory). No decompiler output was used.

## Traps

- **Unreferenced members of an anonymous-namespace class are not emitted.** Defining `CalcEntrance` and `AssignControllers` out of line added nothing to the object, because they have internal linkage and their only callers (`Calc`, `ShowMultiPlayerEntrance`) are not yet defined. To test such a function, reference it temporarily from an emitted function, and do not commit that reference. Check `nm` for the symbol before you trust an unchanged objdiff count.
- **An inline virtual of an interface can sit in another file's block.** `0x80093630` sits between `Draw` and its helpers, but it is the `IGameMap` slot 15 default. This file's copy is kept because `CDlgPiiSelector.cpp` links before `CGameEnding.cpp` and `CGameMap.cpp`. Check the word against the interface vtables before you assign it to the selector.
- **A declared-only virtual hides the whole vtable.** With `Calc` declared but not defined, `CalcView` and `CalcMtx` defined in the class body compiled to nothing, because `Calc` became the key function and the vtable stayed undefined. Declaring the undefined virtuals `inline` emits the vtable and every defined virtual. Check `nm` for a `V __vt__` entry when defined virtuals of a class are missing from the object (F-93-4).
- **Git Bash `sed -i` converts CRLF files to LF.** A one-line `sed -i` on `CDlgPiiSelector.cpp` silently removed every carriage return. Edit with the editor tool, or restore the line endings afterwards.
- **`ninja` targets on Windows use backslash paths.** `ninja build/wpse-v1/src/game/CDlgPiiSelector.o` reported `no work to do` for a changed source. `ninja 'build\wpse-v1\src\game\CDlgPiiSelector.o'` builds it.
- **Destruction order shows block scope.** `CDataPerPlayer::Calc` scored 93% until the selected-item `shared_ptr` and its cast copy were put in a nested block; the original destroys them before the `CUIManager` call. When only the position of a `__dt` call differs, add a scope before reordering statements (F-93-5).
- **A skipped member shifts every later offset.** The first build put the controller word at 0xBC instead of 0xC4, because the 8 bytes after the `shared_ptr<BUILayout>` at 0xB4 are a member the constructor never writes. Check gaps between consecutive constructor stores in `0x800952B0` (F-93-5).
